FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how Assetsware tracks, borrows, inspects, and maintains your assets.

What is Assetsware?

Assetsware is a QR-code-based asset tracking and management system that lets you track, borrow, inspect, and maintain physical assets from a web dashboard or the Android mobile app.

How does QR code asset tracking work?

Each asset receives a unique QR code label printed straight from the system. Staff scan the code with the mobile app to instantly see details, history, location, and status — no manual data entry required.

Do I need special hardware to scan asset codes?

No. Any Android phone with a camera can scan asset codes using the Assetsware mobile app. Codes are printed as standard sticker labels.

What kinds of assets can I track?

Everything from laptops, lab equipment, and vehicles to furniture and office supplies. The system ships with more than 3,700 pre-loaded standard categories and supports custom categories.

How does recurring maintenance scheduling work?

Assign a schedule (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom) and Assetsware computes the next inspection date automatically. Inspections include condition ratings, checklist tasks, and photo evidence.

Is my asset data secure?

Yes. Data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure with industry-standard encryption, and access is controlled through user roles. See our Privacy Policy for details.
